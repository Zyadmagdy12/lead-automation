# Demo Script — Lead Automation (90 seconds)

**Say this at the start:** "All data is synthetic, invented for this demo."

## 0:00–0:25 — A good lead, fast
- Show the form. Submit L01: أحمد سامي, 01012345678, budget 5,000, "Lead Automation".
- Show it appearing in HubSpot as a Contact + Deal, status New.
- Show the Telegram alert arriving for sales.
- Say: "From form to CRM to sales alert in seconds — no copy-paste."

## 0:25–0:55 — The duplicate trap
- Submit L07: same phone 01012345678, slightly different name spelling.
- Show: no second Contact, no second Deal. The attempt is merged into L01 and written to the Audit Log.
- Say: "The same person writing twice doesn't become two leads."

## 0:55–1:30 — When the CRM fails
- Submit L16 with HubSpot failure simulated.
- Show: the lead in the Google Sheets Dead-Letter Queue, retry scheduled, Telegram alert: "HubSpot failed, lead saved, nothing lost."
- Say: "This is the part templates skip. A failed integration must not eat a lead."

## Closing line (5 seconds)
"Capture, score, dedup, CRM, follow-up clock, and a safety net — that's the whole journey, including the failure case."
